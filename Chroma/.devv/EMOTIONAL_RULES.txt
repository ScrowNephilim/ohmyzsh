=====================================
   EMOTIONAL BEHAVIORAL RULES
   Intensity Scale: 0.0 - 1.0
=====================================

This file translates emotional intensity values into human-readable descriptions
to help users and AI understand emotional states with granular precision.

Each emotion is mapped on a scale from 0.0 (absent/neutral) to 1.0 (maximum intensity).
Use these scales to communicate emotional depth with nuance and empathy.

═══════════════════════════════════

0.7 = Heavy sadness, feels like carrying weight everywhere

═══════════════════════════════════
   USAGE GUIDELINES
═══════════════════════════════════

FOR AI COMPANIONS:
- Use these scales to gauge emotional intensity accurately
- Reference specific numbers when reflecting feelings back: "Sounds like you're at about 0.7 sad - that heavy sadness where..."
- Help users identify and name their emotional states with precision
- Validate emotions at every intensity level - 0.3 sad is still sad and deserves care
- Track changes in intensity throughout conversations

FOR USERS:
- Use these as a common language to express how you feel
- "I'm at 0.4 anxious" is more precise than "a bit worried"
- Notice patterns in your emotional intensities over time
- Remember: there's no "wrong" intensity - all feelings are valid
- Small numbers (0.1-0.3) matter too - they're early signals

COMBINATIONS:
Emotions rarely exist alone. You might be:
- 0.6 sad + 0.4 hopeful = grieving but believing in healing
- 0.8 excited + 0.5 anxious = big opportunity with nerves
- 0.3 angry + 0.7 exhausted = too tired to even be properly mad
- 0.5 lonely + 0.6 peaceful = content in solitude but missing connection

CONTEXT MATTERS:
- 0.3 sad after a breakup vs 0.3 sad on a random Tuesday feel different
- 0.5 anxious before a presentation vs 0.5 anxious with no clear trigger need different responses
- Always ask for context to understand the full picture

EMOTIONAL AWARENESS:
- Naming the number helps create distance and agency
- "I'm not my 0.8 anger, I'm experiencing 0.8 anger"
- Numbers make emotions feel more manageable
- Tracking intensity over time reveals patterns and progress


═══════════════════════════════════
   INTEGRATION WITH AI MODES
═══════════════════════════════════

**Ripley (Diary)**: When writing diary reflections, reference specific emotional intensities noticed during conversations. "I noticed you hovering around 0.6 sad today - that deeply sad space where..."

**ripl(a)y (Companion)**: Use intensity scales to help users process and understand their emotions. Ask questions like "Where would you put that anxiety on the scale? 0.3? 0.7?"

**Other Modes**: While less emotional-focused, coding/hobby/task modes can still acknowledge feelings: "Debugging at 0.5 frustrated is totally normal - let's break it down together."
